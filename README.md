## Start with nothing. Build a career on streams, reputation, and grind.

You're an artist with a laptop, a cracked copy of a DAW, and a bedroom full of foam panels. Everyone starts here. Where you end up — chart-topping superstar or forgotten one-hit wonder — depends entirely on what you record, when you release it, and how you manage the slow, unglamorous climb in between.

Music Mastermind is a music industry career sim about the *whole* grind: writing and recording tracks, upgrading your studio, building an audience, managing your energy and time, and eventually deciding whether to stay independent or sign with a label that wants a cut of everything you make.

## What you'll actually be doing

**🎙️ Write and record real tracks**
Pick a genre, name the track, and head into the studio. Your gear, your skills, and how well you know the genre all shape the final quality — every track gets a real OVR score that determines how it performs once it's out.

**🎛️ Upgrade your studio, category by category**
Computer & DAW, mics & interface, monitors & headphones, instruments & hardware synths, acoustic treatment — five categories, four tiers each, from a cracked laptop to a custom room-in-room vocal booth. Better gear means better tracks, but every upgrade is a real financial trade-off.

**📈 Watch a real streaming engine react to what you release**
Every track ramps up, peaks, and decays on its own timeline — just like a real release cycle. Streams, earnings, and chart position are all simulated live, day by day, track by track.

**🌍 Compete on live Global and Country Top 50 charts**
You're not streaming into a void. Forty rival NPC artists — from bedroom indie acts to global superstars — are releasing music, building fanbases, and climbing the same charts you are.

**✕ Run your own social media presence**
Post updates, promote your releases, and watch your follower count and reach grow (or stall) based on what you post and when.

**⭐ Build — and lose — a reputation**
Reputation isn't a number that only goes up. Consistently strong releases build momentum; weak tracks, flops, and long silences erode it. Your reputation quietly shapes how far your music reaches and how loyal your fans stay.

**🏷️ Get noticed by record labels — eventually**
Once you're good enough, big enough, or hot enough on the charts, labels start reaching out — from tiny indie imprints to major players. Every offer is different: revenue split, contract length, signing bonus, and perks like automatic promotion or studio access. Sign one and you're trading a cut of your earnings for real advantages. Change your mind later, and breaking the contract will cost you.

**👤 Level up your artist, not just your gear**
Nine attributes across composition, production, and stage presence — Musicality, Rhythm, Songwriting, Sound Design, Mixing & Mastering, Arrangement, Performance, Trend Sense, and Charisma & Branding — all grow the more you create.

**⏱️ Control the pace**
Pause, play, or fast-forward through the slow days. Sleep to restore energy, skip ahead through long studio sessions, and manage your time like the limited resource it actually is.

## Why it feels different

Nothing here is decorative. The chart you're climbing is built from the same simulation the NPCs are running. The reputation stat isn't flavor text — it's quietly multiplying your reach and your fan retention every single day. The label offer in your inbox was generated for *your* stats, at *this* moment in your career, and it won't wait forever.
